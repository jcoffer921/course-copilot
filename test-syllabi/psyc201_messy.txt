PSYC 201: Cognitive Psychology
Department of Psychology

Instructor: Prof. L. Okonkwo
Term: this semester

Course Overview:
This course surveys core topics in cognitive psychology including memory,
attention, perception, language, and decision-making. We will read primary
research literature and discuss experimental methods throughout.

Unit Schedule:
  Unit 1 - Attention and Perception
  Unit 2 - Memory Systems
  Unit 3 - Language Processing
  Unit 4 - Judgment and Decision-Making
  Unit 5 - Problem Solving
